Fereshteh is a Ph.D. student in Geotechnical Engineering at Virginia Tech. Her research interests include ground improvement, numerical modeling of geotechnical systems, unsaturated soil behavior, and slope stability. She is particularly interested in using advanced computational methods, including the Material Point Method, to study large deformation problems and the response of soils under environmental and mechanical loading. Her previous research has focused on deep soil mixing for liquefaction mitigation and the microstructural and mechanical characterization of soils and rocks. Outside of her academic work, she enjoys spending time in nature, hiking, mountain climbing, and practicing yoga. She also enjoys exploring new places, meeting people from different backgrounds, and maintaining an active and balanced lifestyle.
