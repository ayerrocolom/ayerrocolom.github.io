Mike is a PhD student in civil (geotechnical) engineering at Virginia Tech. His research involves assessing the deformation of subgrade soils beneath temporary roadway panels, with intended uses in military operations and disaster relief efforts. When complete, the work will combine numerical analysis, constitutive modeling, and cross-referencing with empirical correlations. Mike is completing this work in collaboration with US Army Corps of Engineers researchers at the Corps’ Engineer Research and Development Center (ERDC) in Vicksburg, Mississippi.
Prior to his PhD studies, Mike spent 6 years in geotechnical practice, working primarily on transportation and infrastructure projects. He is a licensed PE in New Jersey and Pennsylvania. Outside of work, Mike writes and presents on various topics within the history of geotechnical engineering. His blog on the subject for ASCE’s Geo-Institute is available at: (https://geoinstitute.org/taxonomy/term/7982)
