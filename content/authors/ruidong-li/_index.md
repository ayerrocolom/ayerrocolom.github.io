Francis is a Ph.D student in Civil Engineering at HKUST and co-supervised by Prof. Alba Yerro at Virginia Tech. His research focuses on the investigation of the cone penetration tests in unsaturated soil. His work involves centrifuge experiments, numerical modelling and analytical interpretations of the effect of matric suction on the response of CPT.
