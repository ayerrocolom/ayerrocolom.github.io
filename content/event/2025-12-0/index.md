abstract: 'Abdel successfully defended his PhD dissertation titled *“Simulating earthquake-triggered runout using higher-order hydromechanical MPM and PM4Sand”* at Virginia Tech.

His research focused on understanding earthquake-triggered landslides and runout processes using advanced numerical modeling, combining hydromechanical material point methods with constitutive modeling for liquefiable soils. The work provides valuable insight into how complex coupling between shaking, pore pressure generation, and large deformation controls post-earthquake ground failures.

It has been a great and productive journey since he started, and his contributions have significantly advanced the field of dynamic landslide modeling using the Material Point Method.

Congratulations, Abdel, on this outstanding achievement and best wishes for the next chapter!'
