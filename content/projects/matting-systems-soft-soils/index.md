## Project Information

- **Period:** 2024-2027
- **Role:** Principal Investigator
- **Sponsor:** U.S. Army Engineer Research and Development Center (ERDC)
- **Total award:** $287,812

## Overview

Numerical investigation of matting system performance on soft soils for mobility and infrastructure applications.

This project contributes to the group’s broader research mission in large-deformation geomechanics, geohazards, and soil–water–structure interaction.
