## Project Information

- **Period:** 2022-2025
- **Role:** Co-Principal Investigator
- **Sponsor:** US Department of the Army, Automotive Research Center

- **Portion:** Yerro’s portion: $195,181

## Overview

Modeling tire–mud interaction using SPH-FEA techniques supported by experimental validation.

This project contributes to the group’s broader research mission in large-deformation geomechanics, geohazards, and soil–water–structure interaction.
