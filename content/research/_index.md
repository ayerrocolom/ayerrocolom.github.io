        Our research focuses on large-deformation geomechanics and water–soil–structure interaction. We develop computational methods to understand and improve the performance, resilience, and safety of geotechnical systems and civil infrastructure. By advancing predictive capabilities for complex geotechnical processes, we support the design and management of safer, sustainable, and more resilient infrastructure systems.
